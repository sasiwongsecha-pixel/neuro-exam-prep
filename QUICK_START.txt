❓ FAQ:

Q: Why is there a hamburger menu on mobile?
A: On small screens (< 768px), the sidebar collapses into a menu to save space.
   Just tap the ☰ icon to open/close.

Q: How do tables look on phones?
A: They automatically stack into a readable format. Wider tables scroll horizontally.

Q: Can students edit the content?
A: No — they can only view. You edit via GitHub's web interface.

Q: Is it secure?
A: Yes! HTTPS by default, all processing in the browser, no data collection.

Q: Can I use a custom domain?
A: Yes! Add it in Settings → Pages → Custom domain (requires DNS setup).
