Brief Examination — Normal Findings

DomainDocumentation Reference
LOCAlert, oriented to T/P/P
MotorNormotonia, Power Gr V, No drift
Reflex2+ symmetric, Babinski absent

💡 Exam Pearl: History + PE must be completed within 25 minutes. Fundoscopy is mandatory for all headache cases.
⭐ UMN vs LMN Tip: Wasting + Fasciculations + Areflexia = LMN. Spasticity + Hyperreflexia + Babinski = UMN.

📋 Long Case Approach

Localization First

Always identify the level before the differential:

  • Cortex/Subcortex
  • Brainstem/Cerebellum
  • Spinal Cord
  • Peripheral Nerve / NMJ / Muscle

🏃 Movement Disorders

Parkinsonism Diagnosis (MDS 2015)

Bradykinesia is MANDATORY + either Tremor or Rigidity.

Red Flags: Early falls (<1yr), rapid progression, poor levodopa response.

🧬 Pathophysiology & Imaging

Stroke Cascade

Penumbra: CBF 10-20 mL/100g/min. Reversible tissue target for tPA/Thrombectomy.

Core: CBF <10. Irreversible damage (Cytotoxic edema).
