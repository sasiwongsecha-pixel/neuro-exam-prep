## 🆘 STUCK? HERE'S HELP

**Can't upload the file?**
- Make sure you're on the repository page
- Look for the green "Add file" button
- If it's not there, go to: github.com → your username → neuro-exam-prep

**Website shows a blank page?**
- Hard refresh: Press **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac)
- Wait 2 minutes (GitHub needs time to publish)
- Try a different browser

**Hamburger menu doesn't work?**
- Hard refresh your page
- Clear browser cache
- Try on a different device

**Can't find your URL?**
- Go to: Settings → Pages
- It should show your website URL there
