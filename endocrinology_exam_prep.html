📋 Quick Reference — Key Clinical Clues
⚠️ Red Flags / Don't Miss
  • Rapid onset Cushing → suspect ACC or ectopic ACTH
  • Cushing + virilization → ACC until proven otherwise
  • T >200 ng/dL → androgen-secreting tumor
  • Pituitary apoplexy → thunderclap headache + visual loss
  • Adrenal crisis → treat BEFORE waiting for labs
  • Short stature + virilization → CAH
🔬 Pathognomonic Signs
  • Looser zones → Osteomalacia
  • Café-au-lait "Coast of Maine" → McCune-Albright
  • Blue sclera → Osteogenesis imperfecta
  • Bitemporal hemianopia → Pituitary macroadenoma
  • Archibald sign → AHO / Turner syndrome
  • Trident hand → Achondroplasia
